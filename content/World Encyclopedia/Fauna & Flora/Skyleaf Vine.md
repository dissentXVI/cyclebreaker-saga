The Skyleaf Vine is a restless plant that grows almost exclusively at height — cliff edges, tower parapets, the crowns of ancient trees — anywhere the wind moves freely and the ground feels far away. Its tendrils twist toward air currents even when the atmosphere is still, its seeds disperse erratically rather than following predictable patterns, and in captivity it tends to wilt and stall without the stimulation of open sky. Given patience and a gentle hand, it can be coaxed into controlled growth, but force only makes it wither faster. In [[Aqualonde]], the [[The Tree Shapers Guild|Tree Shapers]] associate it with youth and restlessness — the impulse to seek high places and resist anything that feels like being contained — spreading wide, reaching far, but rarely settling long enough to put down real roots.
