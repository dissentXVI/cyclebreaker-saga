Springbreath is a gentle yellow-petaled flower with a slender green stem, outwardly unremarkable save for the faint luminous mist it emits during blooming season — a fine pollen cloud that refracts light like a soft haze and infuses anything it touches with Life [[pneuma]]. Contact or inhalation produces a soothing effect on mind, body, and spirit, and alchemists regard it as more efficacious than common healing herbs for the brewing of restorative potions. Once reasonably common across [[the Fauconwood]], it has grown rarer in recent generations. Emperor [[Thallan Lael'Nathor]] designated it as his contribution to the imperial arboretum in one of his last official acts, and the choice was understood by contemporaries as emblematic of his reign — a healing gesture from a man who styled himself a restorer, and who wrote of his hope that the [[Navarresean See]] might one day be a land where all peoples lived as freely and gently as a meadow in full bloom.
