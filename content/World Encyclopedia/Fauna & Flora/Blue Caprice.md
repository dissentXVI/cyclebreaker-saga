Blue Caprice is a wildflower bearing purple stems and blue petals that make it nearly indistinguishable from [[Titan's Reach]] at a glance — a resemblance that has caused more than a few foragers an unpleasant afternoon. The critical difference lies in the petal count: Blue Caprice always presents an even number, where Titan's Reach does not. Its oils carry a concentration of Death [[pneuma]], mildly disruptive on its own but a potent enhancer of poisons when properly extracted, and it serves as the primary ingredient in shrinking potions. Emperor [[Isandor Vael'Dorath|Vael'Dorath]], the self-styled Wyrmslayer, dedicated it to the imperial arboretum in the wake of the First Celestial Campaign with a martial flourish — framing its disruptive properties as a fitting tribute to his conquests and a poison for the empire's enemies.
