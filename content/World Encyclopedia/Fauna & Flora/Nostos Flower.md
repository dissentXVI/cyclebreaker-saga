The Nostos Flower is a small, plain blossom — white petals on short green stems — whose only remarkable quality is also its most inexplicable: its petals shift color in response to the emotions of nearby living creatures. This reaction operates through atma, a subtle force distinct from [[pneuma]] that is easily drowned out by ambient magic, which also renders the flower useless as an alchemical ingredient. It has no medicinal value and no practical application whatsoever. Empress [[Eron Asj'Norriah]] chose it anyway for her imperial arboretum dedication, a decision her successors and contemporaries found puzzling — whatever personal significance it held for her was never recorded, the bulk of her writings now lost. The flower is effectively extinct in the wild, making the surviving cultivated specimens among the last of their kind and the empress's inner life, like the plant itself, largely inaccessible to those who came after.
