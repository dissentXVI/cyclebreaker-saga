The Ironwood is among the slowest-growing plants in Aspiria, adding mass and density over decades until its bark blunts most blades and its trunk can weather storms that topple everything around it. What it gains in resilience it costs its environment — its root system is extensive and demanding, drawing nutrients so thoroughly from the surrounding soil that neighboring plants struggle to survive, leaving old Ironwood groves as islands of endurance circled by thinned, worn-out earth. In some sacred sites across [[Halinor]], ancient specimens have stood through [[Cataclysm|Cataclysms]] and wars and are revered for that survival alone. In [[Aqualonde]], the [[The Tree Shapers Guild|Tree Shapers]] hold an honest ambivalence toward it: a plant that weathers everything, but rarely leaves room for anything else.
