Caelynn's Bloat is a bulbous, unsettling flower that swells with internal sacs secreting a slow-acting poison over the course of its growth, its coloration deepening from pale lavender to vivid aubergine as the toxin reaches full potency. Despite its grotesque appearance, the plant is nearly scentless and almost tasteless, making it deceptively easy to conceal in food or drink — though it can also cause harm through inhalation or skin contact. This combination of qualities has earned it a long, quiet history in court assassination and succession disputes across [[Halinor]]. Emperor [[Phaendra Yin'Balar]] elevated it to the imperial arboretum with unusual fanfare, his dedication plaque the most overwrought in the collection — laden with religious titles and proclamations of divine favor — leaving scholars to debate whether the choice was theological symbolism, a private warning to rivals, or simply a powerful man's taste for the theatrical.
