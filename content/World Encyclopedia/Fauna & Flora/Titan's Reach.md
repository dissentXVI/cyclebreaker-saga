Titan's Reach is a tall wildflower distinguished by its deep purple stem and broad blue petals, always appearing in an odd number — a key distinction from its deceptive lookalike, the [[Blue Caprice]]. It grows most abundantly in areas rich with Life [[pneuma]], drawing that energy upward into a concentrated oil within its stem that encourages physical growth and fortitude. Alchemists prize this oil as a primary ingredient in potions of growth, and the Navarresean commander [[Zumrah]] reportedly maintained a steady diet of the flower among his soldiers to produce fearsome physical specimens. A recovered rhyme from his arboretum dedication encodes the odd-petal rule as a soldier's mnemonic — a small piece of battlefield wisdom that has outlasted the empire that coined it.
