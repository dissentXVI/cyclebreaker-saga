Princess' Rest is a thorny, sprawling vine native to the colder northern reaches of [[Halinor]], where it winds through evergreen undergrowth and clings to stone with an almost deliberate persistence, its broad waxy leaves staying green long after surrounding plants have surrendered to frost. It is not simply hardy — it is subtly animate, drawn toward Life [[pneuma]] and feeding on it through the contact of its thorns, which simultaneously lacerate and absorb. Fae stories tie the plant to a cursed sleeping princess sealed inside a tower of thorns to await rescue — a tale whose details shift by region but whose central image endures. The dedication in the [[Navarresean See|Navarresean]] arboretum is the most personal of the collection: an unsigned plaque written by a devoted son on behalf of his mother, Empress [[Amastacia Lael'Nathor|Amastacia]], whose service was repaid with contempt. The tone is quietly furious, and the choice of a choking, thorned vine feels less like a botanical entry than a final word.
