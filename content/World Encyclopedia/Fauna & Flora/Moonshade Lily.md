The Moonshade Lily keeps its petals sealed shut during daylight, opening only after dark — and, according to enduring local legend, in the presence of certain fae songs. Even closed, it emits a strong fragrance that shifts noticeably depending on the quality and composition of the soil it grows in, making two specimens from different locations smell entirely unlike one another. It thrives best in places marked by stillness: forgotten gardens, quiet hollows, gravesites. In [[Aqualonde]], [[The Tree Shapers Guild]] describes it as a plant for the patient and the experimental — one whose fullest potential is missed entirely by anyone unwilling to try different conditions or wait long enough to see what it becomes.
