The Stargazer Orchid is a pale golden bloom that rarely grows in isolation — where one takes root, others follow, spreading outward until whole sections of meadow glitter with them. On clear nights, their petals orient to mirror the constellation patterns overhead, a behavior that has fascinated naturalists and pneuma theorists alike without yielding a satisfying explanation. Despite this collective resilience, individual plants are delicate, and the slightest touch causes their petals to fold shut, rarely reopening. In [[Aqualonde]], the [[The Tree Shapers Guild|Tree Shapers]] regard the flower as a symbol of the tension between collective strength and individual fragility — beautiful and powerful in numbers, but prone to losing themselves in the sky above rather than tending to the ground beneath.
