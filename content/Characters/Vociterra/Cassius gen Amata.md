**Title**: Legatus; formerly Tribunis

**Allegiance**: [[Vociterra]]

**Race**: Human (Vociran)

**Gender**: Male

**Age**: Late 30s at time of death

**Eyes**: Dark brown

**Hair**: Black, short and military-cropped, growing unkempt in his final months

**Attire**: Vociran battle-plate — black iron trimmed in imperial red — worn with increasing neglect toward the end; a worn mourning sash at the shoulder in place of campaign honors

**Home**: Patria Noster

**Status**: Deceased

## Appearance

Cassius gen Amata had the build of a soldier who earned his rank through campaigns rather than connections — broad-shouldered, well-scarred, and carrying himself with the hard confidence of someone who has been trusted with the lives of others and not always brought them home intact. His features were sharp and Vociran in cast: strong jaw, close-cropped black hair, dark eyes that colleagues once described as focused and subordinates later described as hollow. The grim lines of his face were noted even by those who served under him loyally. In his final months, discipline eroded visibly. His armor went unpolished. The mourning sash he wore after [[Megaera cen Magellus|Megaera]]'s death was never removed. Those who encountered him near the end reported that he looked less like a soldier and more like a man conducting a siege against himself.

## Personality

In his prime, Cassius was ambitious, exacting, and difficult to ignore. He was a capable tactician and a demanding commander, driven by the particular hunger of someone who had fought harder than those above him and believed the gap between his position and his merit was a wound that only action could close. He was not cruel by nature — but he was calculating, and the line between the two became harder to find the further his career advanced.

After the [[The Skirmish of Dagger Canyon|Skirmish of Dagger Canyon]] and the death of his sister, that calculating quality curdled into something ungovernable. Grief broke open whatever discipline had kept his ambition in check, and what remained was rage without direction — which made him uniquely vulnerable to [[Kyrichos]], who supplied both a target and a method. By the time Cassius led his men into the caves beneath [[Sticaea Lake]], the man who had once wanted to surpass [[Gallus gen Quietus|Gallus]] had long since stopped caring whether he survived.

## Narrative Relevance

Cassius gen Amata is the direct cause of the [[The Trembling Foundations|Trembling Foundations]] — the cataclysm that reawakened [[Krisnarax]], drained Sticaea Lake, and raised [[The Cosmic Pillar|the Watcher of the Lake]] from beneath the earth. His unauthorized incursion into [[Tarwa'kem]] territory during the [[The Skirmish of Dagger Canyon|Skirmish of Dagger Canyon]] also resulted in the death of [[Shakir Karo'kem|Sultan Shakir]], destabilizing Tarwa'kem's succession and creating the political vulnerability that [[Vociterra]] later exploited. Though a minor figure in the Empire's accounting, Cassius's grief-driven choices set in motion consequences that are still unfolding. [[Kyrichos]] chose him deliberately. That choice has never been publicly examined.
