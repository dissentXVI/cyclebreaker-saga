## Appearance

Haxidemos cuts an unsettling figure — tall, composed, and deliberately obscured. His robes are black silk, finely made, and his mask and effects carry the same dark, polished quality, as though every detail of his appearance has been chosen to project authority rather than conceal vulnerability. His eyes, a cold blue visible through the mask's openings, are the only feature that can be reliably described. He moves without urgency and occupies space with the particular ease of someone who has never had reason to look over his shoulder.
## Personality

What little has been observed of Haxidemos suggests a man of sharp intelligence and open contempt for failure, inefficiency, and for those he considers beneath his attention, which appears to be most things. He speaks with the arrogance of someone accustomed to operating above consequence, and he does not trouble himself to hide it. He is impatient where his colleague [[Kyrichos]] is methodical, and the friction between them, though tempered by apparent mutual respect, is not difficult to detect. He views the pieces on the board as tools, and tools that underperform are of no further interest to him.
## Narrative Relevance

Haxidemos is one of the [[Archorans]] — robed figures who have been observed moving through the margins of major events without direct involvement. He has been seen in the aftermath of the goblin assault on [[Laconia]], walking among the dead alongside [[Kyrichos]] and speaking about the living as one might discuss a disappointing harvest. The full scope of his agenda, his capabilities, and his relationship to the larger designs of the Archorans is not yet known. He wears black where [[Kyrichos]] wears white. What that difference signifies, if anything, has not been determined.
