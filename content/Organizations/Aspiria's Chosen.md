## Overview

Aspiria's Chosen is the name given to the group of four adventurers — [[Ariesa Hecate]], [[Frog Gulgalla]], [[Gothmog Gansukh]], and [[Laena Zahir]] — who received the Blessing of Aspiria and have since operated at the center of Halinor's most consequential events. The name was not self-appointed. It originated with [[Reyn'lael Joran]], who used it in his journal to refer to the group. Ariesa embraced it, and the name has followed them ever since.

The Blessing of Aspiria is not a grant of power in the conventional sense. It does not bestow spells or elevate combat ability. Those who carry it find themselves resistant to the kind of pneumeric unraveling that has undone other adventurers in their place, and they have demonstrated a capacity to endure — and ultimately overcome — forces that should, by most measures, be beyond mortal reckoning. Whether the Blessing is a protection, a mark, or something more deliberate remains a matter of quiet debate among those few who know enough to debate it.

## History

The four met through circumstance rather than design, drawn together by overlapping threads of need, proximity, and purpose. Their early work centered on [[Laconia]] and its surroundings, where they encountered [[the Fang]], defended the town against a Paragon assault, and first began to draw the attention of forces larger than themselves.

Among the most consequential of their undertakings to date was the expedition into [[the Cosmic Pillar]] — the ancient Navarresean structure that had lain dormant beneath Sticaea Lake for over a thousand years before [[The Trembling Foundations]] brought it to the surface. Within the tower, they navigated sealed chambers, recovered lost history, and ultimately confronted [[the Empty]] — a void-born entity that corrupted [[Erros Opars]] and used the tower's systems as a conduit. They destroyed the shade of Erros in the throne room and helped [[Reyn'lael Joran]] complete the act that sealed the tower's threat and ensured its collapse.

Since then, the group was summoned to [[Tarwa'kem]], where [[Neema Karo'kem|Sultana Neema]] confided her concerns about the looming Vociran threat. She tasked them with uniting the city-states of Halinor to stand against the oncoming invasion.

## Narrative Relevance

Aspiria's Chosen are currently operating across Halinor, visiting each city-state to plead for unification against a common enemy. They are known — or known of — by the major powers, though interpretations of who and what they are vary considerably. To some, they are capable but unremarkable wanderers who happened to be present during significant events. To a growing number, they are a living myth in the process of being written.
